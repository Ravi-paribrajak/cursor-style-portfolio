How I got here

I'm a Computer Science Engineering student at Nalanda College of Engineering in Bihar, India. I write the software and run the strategy for everything I ship, and I film most of it.

My channels are in English and reach viewers well outside India. Software comes first. Hardware is the hobby that keeps the videos fun.

If I can't explain it in a sixty-second video, I haven't finished building it.
